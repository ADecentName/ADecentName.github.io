 Great choice\! You raised awareness while remaining respectful. **🤝 Support \+10**

### **❌Consequence:** Sharing the video exposes more people to the fake content, increasing its reach and causing further harm to your friend’s reputation and emotional wellbeing. **Did you know?**  
Research indicates that 10–40% of young people experience cyberbullying and approximately 50% of victims do not know the identity of their aggressor. This uncertainty increases victims’ fear, distress and feelings of powerlessness.
