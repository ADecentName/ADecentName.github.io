Prototypes

Making reporting easier to reach, and easier to credit

Two static mockups exploring changes to Instagram's reporting flow: adding a SafeSteps attribution to the report confirmation screen, and lifting the report action onto the post itself instead of burying it two menus deep.

Unofficial design mockups for a student project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram or Meta. Nothing here is functional — no form submits, and no data is collected.
